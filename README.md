# 🌱 Carbon Bazaar
![Carbon Bazaar Architecture](public/Screenshot%202026-02-06%20072027.png)
### Where sustainable action becomes real financial empowerment

Carbon Bazaar is a farmer-inclusive digital carbon credit trading platform built to support India’s upcoming **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS – 2026)**. The platform connects regulated industries with Indian farmers in a transparent, compliant, and scalable carbon marketplace.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

India will operationalize its Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) by 2026, covering approximately **800 entities across 9 industrial sectors**. While industrial demand for carbon credits is set to rise sharply, agriculture remains largely excluded from the formal carbon market.

Key challenges:
- Farmers lack awareness, digital access, and technical support  
- Existing platforms are industry-centric and complex  
- No trusted, transparent system connects farmers directly to buyers  
- Exclusion limits carbon supply, farmer income diversification, and climate impact  

There is a critical need for a **farmer-inclusive, user-friendly digital carbon trading ecosystem** aligning:
- Industrial compliance  
- Farmer livelihood security  
- National climate and sustainability goals  

---

## 👥 Customer Segments

### 🏭 Businesses & Industries
- Mandatory carbon credit procurement under CCTS  
- Access to reliable, domestic, agriculture-linked carbon credits  
- Traceability, audit readiness, and strong ESG reporting  

### 🌾 Farmers
- Stable secondary income beyond crop sales  
- Conversion of climate-positive practices into tradable credits  
- Local-language education and AI-based guidance  
- Direct access to verified buyers with fair pricing and timely payments

## 🎯 Value Proposition

### For Businesses
- Compliant, transparent carbon credit marketplace  
- Reduced regulatory, reputational, and intermediary risk  
- Reliable domestic supply of verified credits  

### For Farmers
- New non-crop income stream  
- Education, guidance, and continuous support  
- Trusted platform with clear earnings visibility  

### For the Carbon Market
- Bridges industrial demand with untapped agricultural supply  
- Improves liquidity, credibility, and market depth  
- Simplifies complex carbon market processes  

### For the Nation & Environment
- Encourages sustainable, low-emission farming  
- Strengthens rural livelihoods  
- Supports India’s national climate commitments  

---

## 🧩 Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

- Single platform combining **carbon trading + farmer enablement**  
- Farmers treated as **active market participants**, not beneficiaries  
- Aligns industrial compliance, farmer income, and climate action  
- Built as a scalable foundation for an inclusive Indian Carbon Market

## 🔗 Go-To-Market Strategy

### Farmers
- Partnerships with FPOs, cooperatives, SHGs  
- Tie-ups with agri-tech platforms and NGOs  
- Vernacular digital outreach (WhatsApp, SMS, mobile-first web)  
- On-ground enablement via trusted local networks  

### Businesses
- Direct outreach to CCTS-regulated industries  
- Partnerships with industry associations and ESG consultants  
- Engagement through policy forums and climate-tech events  

---

## 💰 Revenue Streams

- **Transaction-based commission** on completed carbon credit trades  
- **Business subscriptions** for dashboards, analytics, and ESG reporting  
- **Verification and enablement services** in partnership with MRV agencies  

Revenue scales with real market activity while maintaining strong farmer incentives.
